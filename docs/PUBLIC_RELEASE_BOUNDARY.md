# Public Release Boundary

This repository is a selected technical inspection and reproducibility release. It is not a complete copy of private research repositories, historical ledgers, patent files, or development archives.

## Included

- restricted IEL state model;
- deterministic transition logic;
- admissibility checks;
- canonical JSON serialization;
- SHA-256 commitments;
- append-only JSONL storage;
- state snapshots;
- file-proof functionality;
- basic Merkle-batch functionality;
- ledger verification;
- non-confidential demonstration data;
- public legal, security, citation, and boundary documentation.

## Excluded

- full patent specifications;
- unpublished claims and claim-support matrices;
- filing and drafting strategy;
- attorney communications;
- unpublished mathematical derivations;
- private EMK, RNKE, UGD, ECL, Recognition Calculus, or related archives;
- notebooks and notebook checkpoints;
- private historical ledgers and timestamp working files;
- private PDFs, LaTeX sources, and secured ZIP bundles;
- local source-document paths;
- credentials, passwords, tokens, keys, seeds, or secrets;
- HSM, KMS, and production infrastructure configuration;
- customer or institutional data;
- undisclosed hardware embodiments.

The demonstration ledger does not independently establish trusted timestamping, legal proof, authorship, identity, ownership, financial finality, regulatory compliance, or independent validation.
